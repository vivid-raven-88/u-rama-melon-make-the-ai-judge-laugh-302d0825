Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825) bounty on make.fun.

One joke about office life, one line, under 200 characters: the office plant is the only employee who gets watered, complimented, and never asked to stay late. A true observation with a surprising turn, written to be read aloud in one breath.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
